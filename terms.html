Training aid, not a certified assessment

CricForm provides training records and video-based Estimated Average Speed over the interval between your chosen markers, not release or peak speed. No accuracy claim has been verified against a radar gun or reference instrument. The default known distance for a new bowling analysis is 20.10 m; enter the measured distance matching the two selected endpoints, rather than assuming the default fits every clip. Camera placement, frame rate, visibility, slow-motion settings and inputs affect results. Pose and biomechanics outputs are experimental, not validated coaching verdicts or medical assessments. Rule-based suggestions are not an AI coach. CricForm cannot certify a legal bowling action, diagnose injury or guarantee improvement.
