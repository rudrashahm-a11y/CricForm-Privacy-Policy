CricForm account and associated-data deletion

CricForm: Bat & Bowl Analysis, including Pace Lab and Bat Lab. Owner and developer: Rudraksh Shah. Updated 8 November 2026.

Request deletion by email

You can request deletion of your CricForm sign-in account and associated data without installing or signing in to the app. Email rudrakshshah33@gmail.com with the subject CricForm account deletion, preferably from the email address used for your account. If it differs, include only the account email to identify it.

This deletion mailbox is monitored. The developer may need a reply confirming account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents, private keys, videos or other training files. We will complete deletion of your CricForm account within 30 days after your request is verified and confirm completion by email. The confirmation will explain what was deleted and any retained data, its reason and duration. Sending an email does not instantly delete the account or erase your devices; follow the local-data instructions below.

Delete your sign-in account in the app

Open CricForm Settings > Delete account and follow the confirmation. Firebase account deletion needs internet access and may require a recent sign-in. If it fails, sign in again or use the email request above. Deleting your CricForm account does not delete your Google account.

The app then attempts to clear several local web stores. Deletion of every native Bat Lab record, private file, pose cache and preference by this action has not been verified. A confirmation message must not be treated as proof that all local copies were removed.

Remove all associated local data

  1. Complete the account-deletion request before clearing storage, where possible. Export anything you deliberately want to keep first; keeping a backup also keeps a copy of that data.
  2. On each Android device used for CricForm, open Android Settings > Apps > CricForm > Storage, then Clear data or Clear storage (wording varies). This removes app-managed data in that storage, including both labs' records, drafts, preferences, private media copies and analysis caches.
  3. For web use, clear CricForm site data in each browser profile and for each website address/origin where you used it. The Android app and each browser store data separately.
  4. Separately remove original photos/videos, gallery or Downloads files, exported JSON backups, PDFs, CSV files, shared copies and backups in other apps or cloud drives. Empty their trash or backup copies if you also want those removed. App-storage clearing cannot delete these destination files.

Clearing storage or uninstalling alone does not request Firebase account deletion. The developer cannot remotely wipe records held only on your devices. Importing an old backup can restore old records. CricForm does not automatically upload training media, GPS/workout records or pose data for account recovery.

Service retention and remaining copies

CricForm retains small device-local account ownership and deletion-recovery records containing your Firebase user ID and account scope. They do not automatically expire. Clear CricForm app data and the relevant browser site data on each device to remove them. These records do not contain passwords, authentication tokens, training records or videos.

Firebase's published Authentication policy describes IP logs kept for a few weeks and other authentication information removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion is initiated. This provider retention is separate from our commitment to complete the account-deletion action within 30 days after verifying your request. See Firebase privacy and retention.

Google advertising data has its own retention rules; deleting Firebase or resetting the Advertising ID does not erase all historical ad data. Email requests and replies are processed by the support mailbox and email providers. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to verify, process and confirm the request, and to keep records required by law. Any retained legal record is limited to what is necessary for the obligation and kept only while that obligation applies. Files held by recipients remain subject to their control.
