Privacy Policy

CricForm: Bat & Bowl Analysis · Updated 8 November 2026

About this policy and the developer

Rudraksh Shah is the owner and developer of CricForm: Bat & Bowl Analysis, including Pace Lab and Bat Lab (StrokeLab). Contact rudrakshshah33@gmail.com for support and privacy or deletion enquiries. Training analysis is local-first; Firebase Authentication and Google advertising are online services. This policy covers both local processing and data sent by those services.

Account information and Firebase

Google Firebase Authentication processes information needed for sign-in and account security, including your email address, user identifier, provider details, and technical information such as IP address and user agent. Google sign-in may provide a display name and profile picture. Email/password credentials are handled by Firebase; CricForm does not save your password in its training records. Authentication, verification and password-reset requests require a network connection. Google processes this information under its applicable terms and privacy policies.

Training records, photos, videos and pose processing

Profiles, workouts, notes, goals, reviews, analysis results and drafts are stored on your device in browser storage or the Android app database. Selected or recorded videos and stance photos, any audio already present in a clip, GPS run calculations and pose analysis are processed on your device. CricForm does not transmit this training content to Firebase, Google AdMob or an analysis server. Local copies, thumbnails and retained pose landmarks may support replay and analysis. Exporting or sharing is a separate action you choose. Pose measurements are experimental estimates, not identity recognition, verified technique assessments or medical findings.

Location and permissions

Run tracking requests location when you start a run. Positions are used to calculate distance and pace; saved run records contain totals and splits rather than a stored GPS route. Camera access is requested for recording or capture. Notifications and sound are optional controls. Media import and export use Android file pickers and supported media-saving APIs; access is limited by your device permissions. You can refuse or revoke permissions in Android Settings, but the associated feature may not work.

Advertising and SDK data (Google AdMob)

Production Android advertising is limited to App Open ads when opening or returning to the app and interstitial ads at natural breaks, with no banner placement. The production policy excludes personalised ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 automatically collects and shares network IP addresses, which can reveal approximate location; device or account identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, app set ID and applicable identifiers for signed-in device accounts; usage events such as launches, taps and video-ad views; and performance diagnostics such as startup delays, hangs and energy use. Google uses this data for ad delivery and measurement, SDK analytics, security and fraud prevention, even without a separate analytics SDK. Restrictions can limit identifiers and processing; non-personalised or limited ads do not mean no data collection. CricForm does not attach local training records, media, GPS routes or pose landmarks to ad requests. Google's SDK disclosure is https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure and its privacy policy is https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising restrictions and privacy choices

CricForm does not collect ages or dates of birth. The production policy applies Google's under-age-of-consent treatment (TFUA true) separately to Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for all users, with a maximum ad-content rating of G. This is a conservative ad restriction, not an age check or a Play content rating. UMP does not ask for consent when tagged under age of consent; a consent form is therefore not promised on every launch or in every region. Ads must not be requested when UMP's canRequestAds permission is false. Settings > Ad privacy choices remains the entry point to review available choices; the options shown depend on Google's requirements and configuration. These production settings are being verified for the upcoming release. Android Settings > Privacy > Ads provides system Advertising ID controls; resetting or deleting that ID does not erase all previously processed advertising data.

Sharing and external services

When you export or share a report, image, video or backup, the selected content may include names, notes and performance information. You choose the destination using your device. Recipient apps, email providers and storage services handle the files under their own policies. Backups and reports are not encrypted by CricForm. Website hosting receives normal network requests to deliver pages and model files. Play-installed apps contact Google Play for update checks; this does not upload your training media or records. CricForm has no automatic cloud synchronisation of training records and does not sell them. No separate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics SDK is included; this does not prevent advertising SDK analytics or diagnostics.

Local retention, deletion and exported copies

Local records, saved media copies and analysis caches remain until removed using the relevant controls or cleared from app or browser storage. For account ownership and safe deletion recovery, this device also keeps small records containing your Firebase user ID and local account scope, including after deletion. These records have no automatic expiry; clearing CricForm app data or the relevant browser site data removes them. They do not contain passwords, authentication tokens, training records or videos. Settings > Delete account requests Firebase account deletion and then attempts to clear several local web stores; deletion of every native Bat Lab record, file and preference by that action has not been verified. To remove all associated local data, clear CricForm's storage in Android Settings > Apps > CricForm > Storage > Clear data or Clear storage on each device, and clear site data for each browser origin where you used the web app. This removes app-managed records, drafts, preferences, caches and media copies in that storage. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not request Firebase account deletion. Original recordings, gallery or Downloads files, exported backups, PDFs, CSV files, shared copies and files in another app or cloud drive remain; delete them separately, including trash or backups at those destinations. Export anything you want to keep before deletion; there is no guaranteed cloud recovery.

Account and associated-data deletion request

In CricForm, open Settings > Delete account to request deletion of the Firebase sign-in account; a network connection and recent sign-in may be needed. A failed request is not completed deletion. You can also request account and associated-data deletion without installing or signing in to CricForm: email rudrakshshah33@gmail.com with the subject "CricForm account deletion", preferably from the account email address, and identify the account email only if different. This deletion mailbox is monitored. The developer may need a reply confirming ownership before acting; do not send passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents, videos or private keys. The developer cannot remotely erase local-only records or copies on your devices; follow the local-data instructions above as part of deletion. We will complete deletion of your CricForm account within 30 days after your request is verified and confirm completion by email. The confirmation will explain what was deleted and any retained data, its reason and duration. You can contact support for request status.

Online retention and support correspondence

Firebase retains account information until account deletion is initiated. Its published Authentication policy describes IP-log retention of a few weeks and removal of other authentication data from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion is initiated; this provider retention is separate from our commitment to complete the account-deletion action within 30 days after verifying your request. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Google manages advertising data retention under https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention; deleting a Firebase account does not itself erase Google's advertising records. Emailing support sends your address and message to the support mailbox and email providers so the request can be handled. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to verify, process and confirm the request, and to keep records required by law. Any retained legal record is limited to what is necessary for the obligation and kept only while that obligation applies. Do not include unnecessary personal information.

Audience, age and children

CricForm's intended audience is currently aged 13 and older. It does not collect ages or dates of birth. Parents or guardians should supervise account setup, recording and sharing for young players and ensure applicable account age requirements and permissions are met. Record other people only with appropriate permission. Restricted advertising settings do not establish a user's age or certify Google Play Families compliance; audience declarations and regional obligations must reflect the actual service.

Security and your device

Firebase Authentication and Google Mobile Ads use encrypted network transport (HTTPS/TLS). Local records and exported backups/reports are not given separate file encryption by CricForm. Keep your device and account secure, especially on shared phones; someone who can unlock the device may access its local records. Share only with intended recipients and avoid unnecessary personal information in notes or support messages.

Contact and updates

For CricForm support, privacy questions, access or deletion enquiries, contact the owner and developer, Rudraksh Shah, at rudrakshshah33@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or private keys.
