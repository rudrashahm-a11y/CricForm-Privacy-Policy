Privacy Policy

CricForm: Bat & Bowl Analysis · Updated 20 October 2026

About this policy

This policy describes CricForm: Bat & Bowl Analysis, including Pace Lab and Bat Lab (StrokeLab). Training analysis is local-first. Account authentication and advertising are online services and are not described as offline or anonymous.

Account information and Firebase

Google Firebase Authentication processes information needed for sign-in and account security, including your email address, user identifier, provider details, and technical information such as IP address and user agent. Google sign-in may provide a display name and profile picture. Email/password credentials are handled by Firebase; CricForm does not save your password in its training records. Authentication, verification and password-reset requests require a network connection. Google processes this information under its applicable terms and privacy policies.

Training records, photos and videos

Profiles, training logs, notes, goals, reviews, analysis results and drafts are stored on your device in browser storage or the Android app database. Media you select or record is processed locally. Saved media copies, thumbnails and retained pose landmarks may be stored locally to support replay and analysis. The app does not upload your training videos, photos or pose data to an analysis server. Model-generated measurements are estimates, not identity recognition or medical assessments.

Location and permissions

Run tracking requests location when you start a run. Positions are used to calculate distance and pace; saved run records contain totals and splits rather than a stored GPS route. Camera access is requested for recording or capture. Notifications and sound are optional controls. Media import and export use Android file pickers and supported media-saving APIs; access is limited by your device permissions. You can refuse or revoke permissions in Android Settings, but the associated feature may not work.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Ads appear as a banner near the bottom of the main screen and occasionally as an interstitial at a natural break (never during recording, analysis or results). The AdMob SDK may read your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID), approximate location derived from your IP address, non-precise device information (model, OS version, language), and interaction with the ad. CricForm itself never sends your training records, videos, photos, email address, user identifier or precise location to AdMob. Ad personalisation is handled by Google under the AdMob and Google privacy policies. Where applicable law requires consent for personalised advertising (for example in the EU, EEA, UK and some other regions), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and you can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Without your consent, ads are non-personalised or not shown. You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Google's AdMob privacy information is at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Sharing and external services

When you export or share a report, image, video or backup, the selected content may include names, notes and performance information. You choose the destination using your device. Recipient apps and storage services handle that content under their own policies. Website hosting receives normal requests to deliver the web app and model files. For installs from Google Play, the Android app asks Google Play whether an update is available when it opens. Google Play handles the update under its own policies; training media and records are not sent as part of this check. CricForm does not sell training records, does not include analytics tracking of in-app behaviour, and does not automatically synchronise training records to a cloud account.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local records remain until you delete them, clear the applicable app or browser storage, or uninstall the app. Use the relevant module's deletion controls for its records. Account deletion in Settings requests deletion of the Firebase account and may require signing in again; do not assume that deleting an account removes independently exported files or every module's local records. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings to clear CricForm storage. Delete exported files separately in their destination. Export important records before clearing storage; there is no guaranteed cloud recovery. Firebase retention and deletion are governed by its service policies.

Data deletion request

The quickest way to delete your Firebase account and its data is Settings > Delete account inside the app. If you cannot sign in, email rudrakshshah33@gmail.com from the email address of the account you want deleted, with the subject "CricForm account deletion". We will delete the Firebase account within 30 days and confirm by email. Local training records are stored only on your own device; uninstalling CricForm removes them. The Android Advertising ID is reset or deleted through Android Settings > Privacy > Ads, independently of this app.

Audience, age and children

CricForm shows third-party advertisements and is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not designed for children under 13 and is not a Google Play Families app. If you are under the age required for an advertising-supported service in your region, do not use CricForm. Parents or guardians should supervise account setup, recording and sharing for young players and ensure the applicable account age requirements are met. Record other people only with appropriate permission.

Security and your device

Keep your device and account secure, especially on shared phones. Local storage is not a guarantee against access by someone who can unlock your device. Do not include unnecessary personal information in notes or shared reports.

Contact and updates

For CricForm privacy questions, access or deletion enquiries, contact rudrakshshah33@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or private keys. The date above identifies this version. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy and any required disclosures.
